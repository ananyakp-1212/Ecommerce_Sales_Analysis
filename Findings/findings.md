Business and Scale

The company operates as a multi-category ecommerce retailer, with products spanning categories such as skincare, accessories, shoes, haircare, kitchen, electronics, apparel, and home-related products. The current dataset contains 8,800 customers and 40,000 orders, generating 81,806 order items, or approximately 2.05 items per order.

The available order data covers March 16 to June 14, 2026, representing roughly three months of activity. Since June is only partially represented, comparisons involving June should be treated cautiously.

How Customers Shop

Customers purchase across a broad range of categories rather than concentrating heavily in a single category. Skincare and accessories have the highest number of distinct orders, with 2,110 and 2,079 orders respectively, while bedding has the lowest at 1,658 orders. Overall, the category mix indicates relatively diversified customer demand.

Across the full dataset, there are approximately 4.55 orders per customer. This indicates repeat ordering, although this overall ratio does not mean that every customer places the same number of orders.

Monthly activity provides additional context. April was the strongest month, with 17,200 orders from 5,102 customers. Orders per customer also reached 3.37 in April, compared with 3.05 in March and 2.33 in May. This suggests that April's increase was driven not only by having more active customers, but also by customers placing orders more frequently.

Key Takeaway

The business shows broad-based ecommerce demand and evidence of repeat purchasing. The most notable pattern is the April activity spike, where both the number of active customers and their ordering frequency increased. However, with only around three months of data, longer-term seasonality and customer retention trends cannot yet be established.
